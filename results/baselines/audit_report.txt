7. RefCnt SYNCHRONIZATION semantics and throughput explanation
   RefCnt acquire = non-coherent shared-metadata increment. The
   endpoint only honors the eviction veto after the increment is
   flushed and made visible, i.e. ONE serialized host<->endpoint
   exchange. Modeled as extra_rtt=1 at refcount_op_latency_ns
   (250 ns), NOT zero. Coherence assumption: a hardware-coherent
   metadata region could drop this to 0; we do NOT assume that.

   Why RefCnt / 2Phase normalized valid throughput > 1.0 and > PROSE:
     * Unsafe wastes link bandwidth transmitting STALE payload, so
       its valid goodput is depressed; a correct mechanism that
       transmits only valid bytes can exceed 1.0. (Expected.)
     * RefCnt/2Phase hold a pin from ENQUEUE, so queue-time eviction
       attempts are BLOCKED and the object stays transferable: more
       requests deliver a full valid object. PROSE deliberately
       ALLOWS queue-time reclamation (queue_reclaim=Y) and rejects
       the now-stale descriptor at admission — a correctness-safe
       loss of a few would-be-valid transfers, i.e. a lower valid
       count, hence slightly lower valid throughput.
     * This is a real retention trade-off, not a measurement bias:
       the SAME eviction attempts are offered to all; RefCnt/2Phase
       pay for the higher throughput with a wide pin span (Pin/xfer
       ~2.8-3.0) and by forbidding queue-time reclaim.
